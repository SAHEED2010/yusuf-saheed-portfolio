# Homepage Visual Directions

Recorded: 2026-08-18

Status: **Direction A selected for refinement.** Directions B and C remain research references only.

Prototype question: What visual system should introduce Yusuf Saheed as an engineer, scientific explorer, AI builder, and startup co-founder without looking like a generic developer template?

## Direction A: Signal Lab

- Palette: Carbon `#101214`, Technical green `#176B55`, Signal teal `#46B8B0`, Clean white `#F7FAF8`, Instrument yellow `#FFDD00`. Red is reserved for real warning states inside project evidence, not general decoration.
- Type: Syne for display, Manrope for reading, IBM Plex Mono for evidence and data.
- Layout: Dark evidence-led homepage with a compact header, a portrait-and-thesis hero, an engineering activity rail, and large project evidence bands.
- Signature: A live-looking signal trace connecting identity, projects, research, and the AI assistant.
- Risk: It evolves Victor Eke's dark evidence-first approach and could feel too familiar if the signal language is reduced to decoration.

### Selected refinement

- Keep the evidence-led engineering-instrument character and broaden it beyond a dark developer aesthetic with a complete light theme.
- Use the signal trace structurally: identity flows into activity evidence, verified projects, the Library, impact, and contact routes.
- Add a statistics strip for GitHub activity and dashboard-derived content counts. Unsupported achievement claims remain unpublished.
- Add direct GitHub, LinkedIn, X, email, WhatsApp, Lagos location, and Buy Me a Coffee routes.
- Present contact and social routes as compact icon controls with accessible names and tooltips instead of text-heavy rows.
- Use the recognizable Buy Me a Coffee mark in the yellow support control. Keep the route visible while clearly avoiding any claim that payouts are already enabled.
- Use white and technical green as the dominant light-theme palette. Reserve yellow for the Buy Me a Coffee action and other rare emphasis only.
- Remove the numbered four-area practice strip from the homepage.
- Replace static contribution artwork and fixed coding counters with synchronized GitHub and WakaTime data, including source and refresh state.
- The final product is multi-page: use dedicated routes for work, project case studies, the Library, achievements, about, and contact rather than making the homepage the entire application.
- The homepage may preview those collections, but its navigation and primary actions lead to real route-level pages rather than scrolling through one long SPA.
- Replace the separate top-level Research and Writing labels with the confirmed **Library** architecture.
- Make the opportunity message relevant to recruiters, collaborators, founders, business owners, learners, and science audiences while keeping one clear primary action.
- Preserve teaching, knowledge sharing, and practical impact as a visible part of Yusuf's direction.

## Direction B: Field Notes

- Palette: Laboratory white `#F7F8F5`, Graphite `#171A1D`, Cobalt `#185ADB`, Vermilion `#D94B3D`, Leaf `#2F7D5A`.
- Type: Newsreader for intellectual character, IBM Plex Sans for interface copy, IBM Plex Mono for annotations.
- Layout: Editorial research notebook with a strong nameplate, marginal annotations, publication-style project records, and an index of open questions.
- Signature: Every claim has a visible evidence annotation, making the site feel like a working research folio rather than a resume.
- Risk: Editorial layouts are common; this version must stay specific through mathematical questions, project evidence, and structured annotations rather than newspaper styling.

## Direction C: Build Surface

- Palette: Ink `#111111`, Paper `#FFFFFF`, Electric blue `#2457FF`, Coral `#FF6846`, Mint `#A9E5C8`, Steel `#D8DDE2`.
- Type: Archivo Black for restrained display moments, DM Sans for reading, Space Mono for system labels.
- Layout: Bright product workspace with asymmetric horizontal bands, large product media, a command-oriented assistant, and clear audience routes.
- Signature: The homepage behaves like a controllable work surface where projects, publications, and questions can be filtered by the visitor's intent.
- Risk: Product dashboards can become card-heavy. The direction uses full-width bands, clear dividers, and only framed repeated records.

## Self-critique

The first draft of Direction A used the familiar near-black and acid-green developer palette. It was revised to a broader instrument palette with teal, red, and yellow so the engineering language comes from measurement and signals rather than a terminal theme.

Direction B originally leaned toward warm cream and a high-contrast serif, which is an overused editorial default. It was revised to laboratory white, cobalt, vermilion, and evidence annotations.

Direction C originally resembled a SaaS dashboard. It was revised around full-width work bands and audience-controlled filtering so the structure reflects Yusuf's mixed engineering, research, and product practice.
